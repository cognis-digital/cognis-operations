# Governance

- **Human-in-the-loop:** agents handle high-volume bounded decisions 24/7; humans are final decision-makers
  on anything regulated, ambiguous, or high-risk.
- **Identity & accountability:** every agent has a verifiable identity and a delegation chain anchored to a
  human ([agentpassport](https://github.com/cognis-digital/agentpassport)); every action is logged.
- **Guardrails:** runtime tool allowlists ([toolguard](https://github.com/cognis-digital/toolguard)),
  PII redaction / policy ([guardpost](https://github.com/cognis-digital/guardpost)).
- **Risk & compliance:** mapped to SOC2/ISO/NIST via [compliance-atlas](https://github.com/cognis-digital/compliance-atlas).
- **Observability:** OTel traces + replay ([agentlog](https://github.com/cognis-digital/agentlog)).
